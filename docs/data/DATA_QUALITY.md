# Data quality log

Every finding below was verified on the data; the code that handles it is named. Update this file
whenever a new issue is found (Part 2 will need it).

| # | Finding | Evidence | Handling |
|---|---------|----------|----------|
| 1 | **`customer_service_contacts` is generated by the outcome in training rows.** | Train rows with 0 claims have mean 0.41 contacts, 1 claim 2.96, 2 claims 5.39. Rows with 0 contacts never have a claim; 86% of rows with >=3 contacts do. Spearman with claim count 0.55 / 0.35 / 0.33 (motor/buildings/contents); every other feature < 0.11. In the 2025 test rows the mean is 0.40 (train 0.67) and only 0.8% have >=3 contacts (train 7%), i.e. only baseline contacts. | Excluded (`features.OUTCOME_LEAK_COLS`). With it CV D2 is 0.55; without it 0.10. A model using it would predict ~0 for most test rows. Guard: `drift.target_signal`, test in `tests/test_features.py`. |
| 2 | Supplied `exposure` is corrupted on ~2-3% of training rows. | Full-year rows show 1.0139 (3,037 motor rows) or 365.0 (574 rows). Test exposure is clean. Clean rows equal days/365 (cap 1). | Recompute from dates: `target.recompute_exposure`; mismatches counted in `build_row_target` report. ~12 rows per line have other small mismatches: still to investigate. |
| 3 | 2024 claims are immature. | 20% of 2024 claims open (53% for 2024Q4, 3% for 2023). Motor 2024 loss ratio 0.61 vs 0.81-0.91 in earlier years. Max report lag 1,361 days (bodily injury). The test target is *ultimate* cost. | Not yet handled. To do: development factors by loss quarter and peril, apply to the 2024 target. Fold B (valid 2024) is down-weighted until done. |
| 4 | Recoveries exist only on closed motor own_damage / theft claims, almost always exactly 80% of incurred. | 14,776 claims, ratio 0.80 for 10th-99th percentile; none on open claims. Only 28% of closed own_damage/theft claims carry a recovery (avg 77% of incurred), i.e. 18.5% of incurred on average; those perils are 12% of open 2024 incurred, so unbooked recoveries overstate 2024 motor net cost by ~1.5%. | Net cost = incurred - recoveries (`target.claim_net_cost`). Open own_damage/theft claims will gain recoveries later: part of the development step. |
| 5 | Repudiated claims have zero amounts. | 1,711 claims, all amounts 0. | Excluded. |
| 6 | Extreme claims. | One motor bodily-injury claim of 11.9M (2023); typical 10-20k. Top 1% of rows carry 53% of motor and ~80% of home losses. | Not yet handled. To do: cap / spread large losses in training and measure on CV. |
| 7 | ~400 claims match no policy row. | 274 have 2020 loss dates; the rest fall in gaps between rows. | Dropped and counted (`claims_unmatched`). Still usable for claim-history features. |
| 8 | Weather moves a lot year to year. | 2024->2025 KS shift 0.23 (freezing days), 0.19 (dry summer), 0.18 (temperature / rainfall). | Real signal for the test year; use with care (extrapolation risk). |
| 9 | Fields that look like noise or proxies. | surname, email_domain, vehicle_colour, branch_id, campaign_code, document_preference, marketing_consent, marketing_segment, previous_insurer. | Excluded by default (`features.NOISE_COLS`); to be ablated, not assumed. Also review for fairness / proxy discrimination in the report. |
| 10 | `written_premium` / `discount_applied` reflect the insurer's current pricing. | Allowed as inputs (present in test) but circular: they embed the incumbent model. | Included for now; ablate and report. |
| 11 | **604 duplicate claims.** Exact copies (every field equal except `claim_id`) of an existing claim; the copies have 11-character ids (all others 10), 600 of them have a 2022 loss date. | `claims.claim_id.str.len()==11`: 604 rows, each matches a 10-character original on all 11 other fields; no content duplicates exist among the 10-character ids. They add 1.4% to 2022 claim counts and 1.6% to 2022 cost, and double-count prior claims in the claim-history features of the customers concerned. | Dropped in `cleaning.drop_duplicate_claims` (rule: equal on all fields except `claim_id`, the id length only confirms it). |
| 12 | **252 claims with the placeholder loss date 2020-12-01** (before any policy starts; ~0.3 claims per normal day). | Report dates run through 2021 (and into 2022) with the usual lags to a true loss date inside the first policy year; 229 of the policies start on 2021-01-01. They were unmatched and dropped from the target (0.6% of 2021 claims). 21 more claims dated other days of Dec 2020 and ~140 with loss dates 1-16 months before the policy's first row and long report lags are not repaired (0.1%). | `cleaning.fix_placeholder_loss_dates`: loss_date := report_date - median report lag of the peril, not before the policy's first period start. |
| 13 | **`sum_insured` is 1000x too large on 0.2% of buildings and contents rows** (train and test alike: 1,395 / 1,402 train rows, 370 / 360 test rows). | Values 36M-594M next to a normal range 36k-953k; the premium is unchanged; the same policy's other years are normal; divided by 1000 the flagged rows match the clean distribution (median 222k vs 223k). A tree model puts them in the top sum-insured bin. | `cleaning.clean_policies` divides by 1000 when sum_insured > 20x the table median. |
| 14 | **`construction_type` is recoded and dirty.** T (2021-22) is W (2023+) under a new code (same share, 11.5%); `Brick`/`BRICK`/`brick`, `W`/`w`, `T`/`t`, `stone`/`STONE`, `non_standard`/`NON_STANDARD` are case variants. | Level-by-year table: T has no rows after 2022, W none before 2023; the 2025 test rows contain only W/w. A model trained on 2021-22 (fold A) meets an unseen level in 2023. 11 levels collapse to 4. | `cleaning.clean_policies`: lower-case and merge t into w. |
| 15 | Open claims carry no recoveries yet, but would later. | Closed own_damage claims: 29.5-30.7% have a recovery in every accident year (2024 included), always 80% of incurred; theft 12-15% at 30%. Open claims: 0 recoveries by construction. Expected: 23.6% (own_damage), 4.0% (theft) of incurred. | `target.open_recovery_rates` / `claim_net_cost`: open claims net of the expected recovery (1.37M, 1.2% of motor 2024 cost; local D2 unchanged). |
| 16 | Regions R04 and R05 are the same region under two codes. | Identical yearly weather means on every column and identical address attribute distributions (34,183 and 45,865 addresses). | Left as is (trees and target encoding are unaffected). |
| 17 | Regional claim surge in R07, Oct-Dec 2023 (buildings storm 4x, motor glass_hail 3x, contents flood 1.65x); smaller: buildings storm R01 2022-12. | Monthly counts per peril x region against the cell median (`iaa.events`); no matching signal in the yearly weather. | Kept in the data; thinning for fitting tested (neutral), code behind `--thin-events`. |
