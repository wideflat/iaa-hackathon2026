# Data quality log

Every finding below was verified on the data; the code that handles it is named. Update this file
whenever a new issue is found (Part 2 will need it).

| # | Finding | Evidence | Handling |
|---|---------|----------|----------|
| 1 | **`customer_service_contacts` is generated by the outcome in training rows.** | Train rows with 0 claims have mean 0.41 contacts, 1 claim 2.96, 2 claims 5.39. Rows with 0 contacts never have a claim; 86% of rows with >=3 contacts do. Spearman with claim count 0.55 / 0.35 / 0.33 (motor/buildings/contents); every other feature < 0.11. In the 2025 test rows the mean is 0.40 (train 0.67) and only 0.8% have >=3 contacts (train 7%), i.e. only baseline contacts. | Excluded (`features.OUTCOME_LEAK_COLS`). With it CV D2 is 0.55; without it 0.10. A model using it would predict ~0 for most test rows. Guard: `drift.target_signal`, test in `tests/test_features.py`. |
| 2 | Supplied `exposure` is corrupted on ~2-3% of training rows. | Full-year rows show 1.0139 (3,037 motor rows) or 365.0 (574 rows). Test exposure is clean. Clean rows equal days/365 (cap 1). | Recompute from dates: `target.recompute_exposure`; mismatches counted in `build_row_target` report. ~12 rows per line have other small mismatches: still to investigate. |
| 3 | 2024 claims are immature. | 20% of 2024 claims open (53% for 2024Q4, 3% for 2023). Motor 2024 loss ratio 0.61 vs 0.81-0.91 in earlier years. Max report lag 1,361 days (bodily injury). The test target is *ultimate* cost. | Not yet handled. To do: development factors by loss quarter and peril, apply to the 2024 target. Fold B (valid 2024) is down-weighted until done. |
| 4 | Recoveries exist only on closed motor own_damage / theft claims, almost always exactly 80% of incurred. | 14,776 claims, ratio 0.80 for 10th-99th percentile; none on open claims. | Net cost = incurred - recoveries (`target.claim_net_cost`). Open own_damage/theft claims will gain recoveries later: part of the development step. |
| 5 | Repudiated claims have zero amounts. | 1,711 claims, all amounts 0. | Excluded. |
| 6 | Extreme claims. | One motor bodily-injury claim of 11.9M (2023); typical 10-20k. Top 1% of rows carry 53% of motor and ~80% of home losses. | Not yet handled. To do: cap / spread large losses in training and measure on CV. |
| 7 | ~400 claims match no policy row. | 274 have 2020 loss dates; the rest fall in gaps between rows. | Dropped and counted (`claims_unmatched`). Still usable for claim-history features. |
| 8 | Weather moves a lot year to year. | 2024->2025 KS shift 0.23 (freezing days), 0.19 (dry summer), 0.18 (temperature / rainfall). | Real signal for the test year; use with care (extrapolation risk). |
| 9 | Fields that look like noise or proxies. | surname, email_domain, vehicle_colour, branch_id, campaign_code, document_preference, marketing_consent, marketing_segment, previous_insurer. | Excluded by default (`features.NOISE_COLS`); to be ablated, not assumed. Also review for fairness / proxy discrimination in the report. |
| 10 | `written_premium` / `discount_applied` reflect the insurer's current pricing. | Allowed as inputs (present in test) but circular: they embed the incumbent model. | Included for now; ablate and report. |
